Federal Reserve Simulator
Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Summary

Federal Reserve Simulator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game has no advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or third-party sign-in.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, achievements, preferences, and tutorial progress are stored locally so the game can save and resume. This information is not sent to the publisher. Deleting the game removes locally stored data, subject to normal device-backup behavior on your platform.

Network access

The game does not require a network connection to play and does not send gameplay or diagnostic information to the publisher. On Steam, platform features such as achievements are handled by Steam under Valve’s privacy policy.

Children

Because the game does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

Questions may be sent to billmeadegamingllc@gmail.com. Information voluntarily included in an email is used only to answer the request and is handled by the user’s and publisher’s email providers.

Changes

If the game’s practices change, this policy and its store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.
