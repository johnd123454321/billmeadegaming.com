Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. The game plays fully offline. There are no accounts, ads, or in-app purchases.

Where are my saves?
Progress is saved locally on your device. On Steam, achievements sync through the Steam client.

Is everything unlocked at purchase?
Yes — the full experience is available immediately. There is no additional content to buy.

Refunds
Purchases through Steam follow Steam’s refund policy; purchases through the App Store follow Apple’s refund process.
