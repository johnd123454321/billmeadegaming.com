What’s in the game

Every tool the Fed has. Every mess it made.

  • Guide the U.S. economy across historical and custom scenarios — or step into the decisions faced by landmark Fed chairs.
  • Confront real crises — stagflation, financial panics, recessions, and thoroughly modern disasters.
  • Use the full toolkit — rates, reserve policy, quantitative easing, forecasts, FOMC votes, and carefully worded communications.
  • Live with the lag — track inflation, unemployment, growth, markets, and your credibility as consequences arrive quarters later.
  • Learn as you play — the Fedcyclopedia explains every tool; play the tutorial, focused scenarios, or a long-form sandbox.
  • Fully offline — no accounts, no ads, no tracking. In English, German, Spanish, Chinese, and Brazilian Portuguese.
