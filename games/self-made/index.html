What’s in the game

One life. Every market.

  • A living market — 50+ tradable stocks that trend, crash, gap, and rip through booms and busts.
  • Real options trading — calls and puts priced with an actual Black-Scholes model. Leverage cuts both ways.
  • Run the company yourself — found businesses across five industries, hire, set strategy, or take the CEO chair of your own public company.
  • A whole life to live — homes, real estate, relationships with eleven romanceable characters, marriage, kids, and the hours in the day to juggle it all.
  • Consequences — margin calls, SEC investigations, worker scandals, bankruptcies. The ending you get is the one you earned.
