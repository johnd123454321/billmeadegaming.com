The studio

Small studio.
Serious systems.

Billmeade Gaming is an independent game studio with one obsession: the strange, brutal, occasionally hilarious machinery of money. Markets, monetary policy, leverage, ruin — the systems that run the world make incredible games, and almost nobody builds them.

So we do. In 2026 we shipped three of them:

  • Federal Reserve Simulator (February) — a turn-based monetary-policy strategy game that ended up on public radio’s Marketplace with Kai Ryssdal.
  • SELF MADE: Investment Life Sim (March) — our flagship. A whole investment life, from basement apartment to boardroom, with real options math under the hood.
  • YOLO Mode (April) — a $1.99 arcade shot of pure options-trading adrenaline.

What we believe

  • Systems first. Real mechanics — Black-Scholes pricing, policy lags, credibility — are more fun than fake ones. Depth is the content.
  • Premium and player-owned. Buy the game once, own the game. No ads, no accounts, no season passes.
  • Ship. Small team, fast iterations, games that come out.
